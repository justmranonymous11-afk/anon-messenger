How it's secured
  • Peer-to-peer — the server only introduces you, then steps aside.
  • Messages: ECDH P-256 → AES-GCM 256 on top of the encrypted WebRTC channel.
  • Calls: WebRTC DTLS-SRTP (same crypto family used by major secure messengers).
  • Nothing stored on the server. Messages live only in your browser memory.
